## Ready LinkedIn company-page post — NOT yet scheduled

If you're preparing for a CRA or CTM interview, I recommend starting with the actual job description—not a list of generic interview questions.

Pick one responsibility and ask yourself:
1. What did I personally do?
2. What decision did I make or help support?
3. What happened next, and what can I honestly substantiate?

I've seen how easy it is to describe work at the study level without clearly explaining individual accountability. The solution isn't to inflate experience; it's to connect the employer's requirements to real examples you can defend.
