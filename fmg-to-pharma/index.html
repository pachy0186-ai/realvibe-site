    A few important questions

    What this assessment is—and what it is not.

    Is this only for people who failed to match?

    No. The beta is designed for FMGs who are considering clinical research/pharma as a real career direction, including people who never pursued residency, changed plans, or are genuinely undecided.

    Will this tell me which visa or immigration path to use?

    No. Work authorization is captured only because it can materially change which U.S. roles are realistic. FMG to Pharma does not provide immigration advice.

    Will I be sold a course after I finish?

    No. This research beta does not route you into a predetermined product. The immediate goal is to understand recurring FMG barriers and determine what is genuinely worth building.

    Why ask what I've already spent?

    Because a course, credential, degree, résumé service, or coaching purchase tells us something different from hypothetical interest. The question helps us understand what FMGs have already tried—not to judge your spending.
