Privacy

This page explains, in plain English, what FMG to Pharma collects through the Career Reality Check and how that information is used.

You can view your assessment result without providing your name or email address.

What we collect

The Career Reality Check collects the answers you provide about your professional background, location and general work-access category, residency intentions, career goals, current experience, job-search activity, barriers, and steps you have already tried.

If you choose to provide them, we also collect your first name, email address, consent to receive career resources and updates, and interest in being contacted about a complimentary Career Roadmap Call.

We may also collect the page URL, referring page, and campaign information such as UTM source, medium, campaign, and content parameters.

How we use the information

We use assessment responses to provide the Career Reality Check, understand common career-transition needs, and improve FMG to Pharma resources. We use contact information only when you voluntarily provide it, including to send updates or contact you about a roadmap call when you choose the relevant option.

Aggregate analysis and identifying information

Responses may be analyzed in aggregate to improve FMG to Pharma. Identifying information will not be published without your permission.

Your choices

Providing contact information and selecting either contact option are voluntary. You do not need to opt into email updates or a roadmap call to receive your assessment result.

Please do not submit sensitive identifiers

Do not submit passport numbers, Social Security numbers, immigration document numbers, medical records, employer-confidential information, or other highly sensitive identifiers. FMG to Pharma does not provide legal or immigration advice.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you voluntarily submitted, email fmgtopharma@gmail.com.
