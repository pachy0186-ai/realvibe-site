Research Privacy Notice

This beta is designed to learn about career-transition challenges faced by Foreign Medical Graduates. Participation is voluntary.

What we collect

The assessment may collect your answers about career background, U.S. work-access category, residency intentions, job-search activity, barriers, prior attempts to solve the problem, spending ranges, goals, and urgency. If you choose to provide them, we may also collect your first name, email address, consent to receive resources, and willingness to participate in a research conversation.

Why we collect it

Responses are used to understand recurring FMG career-transition patterns, improve the assessment, decide what educational resources may be worth building, and contact people who explicitly opt into follow-up.

What we do not ask for

Please do not submit passport numbers, Social Security numbers, immigration document numbers, medical records, employer-confidential information, or other highly sensitive identifiers. The assessment is not immigration, legal, recruiting, employment, or medical advice.

Research and reporting

We may analyze responses in aggregate or summarize themes without identifying individual participants. We do not sell assessment responses or contact information to advertisers.

Email and interviews

Email/resource consent and research-interview opt-in are separate choices. You do not need to opt into either to view your assessment result.

Security and retention

Reasonable measures are used to limit access to collected responses. Because this is an early research beta, collection methods and retention practices may evolve; material changes should be reflected in an updated notice before broader public use.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of information you voluntarily submitted, use the FMG to Pharma contact channel through which you received the assessment link.
