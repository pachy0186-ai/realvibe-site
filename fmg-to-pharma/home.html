I took the path I now help other FMGs understand.

I’m Patricia Mieses, MD, MSHS, CCRP—a foreign-trained physician who built my career in U.S. clinical research rather than practicing medicine in the United States.

I started at the investigative-site level as a Clinical Research Coordinator and progressed through Senior CRC, Clinical Trial Manager, and Senior Clinical Trial Manager roles on the sponsor side.

Over 13+ years in clinical research, I’ve worked across clinical trial operations, site feasibility and selection, enrollment strategy, site performance, sponsor/CRO/site collaboration, risk management, inspection readiness, and training.

FMG to Pharma exists because foreign-trained physicians often receive plenty of information about residency, but far less practical guidance about where their medical background actually fits in the life-sciences industry.
