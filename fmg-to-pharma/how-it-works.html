Seven days to an honest application plan.

Each session builds one reusable career-search asset. You'll use current job postings and your own verified experience, rather than a magic ATS score or job guarantee.

Day 1 — Choose the lane

Identify one realistic role family, two alternatives and required eligibility gates.

Day 2 — Decode real openings

Compare actual postings, required experience and qualification gaps.

Day 3 — Translate your background

Create an evidence bank and draft your résumé using only facts you can defend.

Day 4 — Check the application

Review parsing, recruiter-facing qualifications, screening questions and responsible AI use.

Day 5 — Improve visibility

Clarify LinkedIn positioning and prepare relevant, respectful outreach messages.

Day 6 — Rehearse interviews

Practice evidence-based STAR examples, transition answers and honest gaps.

Day 7 — Apply and learn

Submit only suitable applications and set a 30-day evaluation plan.
