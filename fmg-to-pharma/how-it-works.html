CRA Application and Interview Brief

A proposed $399 founder-reviewed concierge brief for experienced CRA applicants targeting one CRA or CRA II opportunity.

Purchases and candidate intake remain closed

One role. Advice grounded in your experience.

  • Your two strongest supported selling points and strongest interview example, with reasons they fit the role.
  • One weaker example and practical advice for improving it.
  • Requirements, evidence gaps, written interview preparation and questions to ask the employer.
  • One 30-minute review conversation and one factual correction pass.

Maximum two participants in the first cohort. First-role exploration, Senior CRA and CTM evaluation, full resume rewrites, mock interviews and unlimited coaching are outside this pilot.

Timing, refund terms and private intake arrangements will be confirmed before enrollment. An inquiry is not an order or reservation. No employment outcomes are guaranteed.
