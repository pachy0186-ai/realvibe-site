The planned pilot process

From a target job to a practical plan.

Start with the role you actually want. Receive focused intelligence you can trace back to sources and your own experience.

Secure order intake is not open yet
01

Share the role and your background

When intake opens, submit the job description or public URL and relevant career evidence through the verified intake channel. Redact personal identifiers and employer-confidential information.

02

Research, map and review

Public-source company research and job analysis are connected to your evidence. Early pilot reports are intended to include founder expert review, with facts, inference and unknowns separated.

03

Act on the priorities

Use a report to focus your application or develop truthful interview examples. The agreed scope, delivery time and data-handling terms will be confirmed before any payment.

What makes an input useful?

A complete job description; your responsibilities and scope; examples you can substantiate; and the specific application or interview decision you need to make. A title alone cannot establish your fit.

What happens when evidence is missing?

The report identifies the missing detail and how to check it. It does not turn adjacent experience into a qualification you do not hold, or assume an employer's internal expectations.

Do I need a call or the Reality Check?

No. Neither is a prerequisite for the planned paid pilot. Calls remain optional. If you are still exploring careers, the Reality Check can help orient that earlier decision.
