Clinical research experience behind the guidance.

Meet Patricia Mieses.

Patricia is a foreign-trained physician who built a career in U.S. clinical research, progressing from investigative-site work to sponsor-side clinical operations and senior trial leadership over more than 14 years. Her background includes an MSHS in Clinical Research Administration and SOCRA CCRP certification.

That site-to-sponsor perspective informs how FMG to Pharma interprets a role: what you independently owned, how study scope changes the work, and which evidence you can truthfully defend.
