Know what to expect

Questions before your next move.

Clear scope and honest limits for the proposed intelligence pilot.

Who are the pilot reports for?

U.S.-market clinical research professionals targeting a specific CRA, Senior CRA or CTM position. You do not have to be an FMG. If you are still choosing a career direction, use the optional free Reality Check.

What does each package include?

Application Intelligence ($49 proposed pilot) focuses on requirements, candidate evidence, truthful positioning and application priorities. Interview Intelligence ($99 proposed pilot) focuses on company context, competency themes, answer development and preparation priorities. Each covers one role at one company.

Is the $397 positioning service another intelligence package?

No. The $49 Application Intelligence and $99 Interview Intelligence pilots focus on one specific opportunity at one employer and are the primary 2.0 offers. The separate $397 Career Positioning & Rewrite Pilot provides more intensive founder-delivered career-transition support for eligible FMGs. It remains available selectively by application inquiry, with scope and availability confirmed before payment.

Can I buy a report now?

Purchases are not open. These prices are experimental. An inquiry does not place an order or reserve a delivery slot. Before payment opens, fulfillment, secure intake, customer terms and payment processing must be verified.

What happens if I save my free Reality Check?

Your result appears on screen immediately, without an email address. Save my response stores the assessment; the optional checkboxes record whether you want occasional resources or contact about a complimentary roadmap call. Saving does not buy a report, book a call, or trigger an automatic results email. If a roadmap invitation appears, use the scheduling link to choose an available appointment separately.

Will I receive an automatic email after an inquiry or payment?

This website currently has no active pilot checkout or automated customer email workflow. An inquiry opens your email app; you decide whether to send it. Before the new packages open for purchase, the payment receipt, customer next-step instructions, founder notification and delivery expectations must be configured and tested.

When would I receive it, and what are the refund rules?

No fixed turnaround or final refund/revision terms have been established. Those terms must be confirmed before enrollment. Do not rely on this pilot for an imminent interview deadline.

How is this different from a generic AI prompt?

The intended difference is traceable public research, clinical-operations interpretation, evidence-to-requirement mapping and prioritized recommendations with expert review. The pilot has not yet demonstrated superiority in a controlled comparison. Read the worked example to assess the approach.

Is the sample a real customer report?

No. It uses real public employer sources and a clearly fictional candidate. The analysis illustrates the intended deliverable; it was not generated by the prototype. Founder quality review is pending.

Do you know the actual interview questions or hiring criteria?

No. Any practice themes are explicitly labeled as inference. Public recruitment guidance may inform preparation, but confidential interview questions, internal decisions and guaranteed outcomes are not claimed.

Will AI and human review be involved?

The planned workflow combines AI-assisted research and analysis with founder expert review during the early pilot. Model-processing consent, provider handling and the review workflow must be verified before accepting candidate documents.

Should I email my résumé now?

No. This website has no active pilot résumé upload or order-intake form. Please send only general questions. Secure intake, consent and a clear retention/deletion practice must be ready before candidate documents are requested.

Is a call required?

No. The existing scheduling link is optional and separate from the paid pilot. The Reality Check is also optional.

Can you guarantee interviews, offers or sponsorship?

No. Reports are educational career guidance. Employers decide who to interview or hire; FMG to Pharma does not provide legal, immigration, recruiting or medical advice.

Are you affiliated with an employer?

FMG to Pharma is an independent RealVibeAI LLC initiative. No pharmaceutical employer or company covered in a report endorses this service.
