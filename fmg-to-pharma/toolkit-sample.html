Day 1 outcome

Stop collecting impressive titles. Select a realistic starting point and a backup. Suggested time: 30–45 minutes.

If you have a medical degree but no U.S. medical license, the job market is not one giant category called “pharma.” Site-based research, CRO operations, and sponsor operations have different hiring filters. Separate the setting from the title.

Three eligibility gates

  1. Required credentials: Does the posting require an active U.S. professional license that you do not hold?
  2. Required research track record: Does it require independent monitoring or years of direct trial operations experience?
  3. Practical eligibility: Can you truthfully meet the employer's stated work authorization, location, travel and schedule requirements?

Job titles vary by employer. Read responsibilities and required qualifications, not acronyms alone.
